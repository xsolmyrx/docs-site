The feature a legal document set actually needs: instead of answering a question, this actively hunts for places where the two manuals define, describe, or handle the same thing differently — contradictions, terminology drift, or gaps between them. Devil's Advocate

Live

Give it a claim or a position, and it looks for the strongest grounded pushback against it — genuine counterpoints from the documents, not manufactured ones. On a real case, this is how you stress-test your own argument before the other side does.

How this works

Each request retrieves the best-matching passages from both manuals (same mechanism as the Search tab), sends only those passages to Claude with instructions to answer strictly from them, and returns a real generated answer with page citations. Nothing is pre-written below this line.

Binder

Collect excerpts from anywhere in this site — Search, Chunk Explorer, Patch Bay, Concept Graph, or any AI Insights citation — into one ordered bundle, then export it as a real PDF. On a real case file, this is how you'd assemble an exhibit bundle from scattered documents.

The exported PDF is a compilation of excerpts you selected, with citations — it is not a legal filing and hasn't been reviewed for accuracy or completeness. Verify everything against the original documents.
